### Gaps

- **Blocking recall and global identity recall:** the candidate list is the only population labelled; there is no gold set of true matches independent of the blocker.
- **Per-path metrics for DeterministicBibliographic and LlmAdjudicated:** no pair took those paths in the sample (zero observations).
- **Calibration (score reliability) and threshold sweep on held-out data:** not done by design: no held-out tuning, and the uncalibrated policy emits no probability.
- **Inter-annotator agreement:** a single reviewer; no second independent label set exists.
- **Real-provider LLM adjudication accuracy:** no real-provider call was made in Phase 5E.

### Threats to validity

- Convenience sample: 300 queued pairs drawn from candidate pairs by oversampled evidence strata, with no population weights. The rates are rates in this sample, not in the corpus. The 5E report states 11,412 decisions in the full candidate population.
- The strata are defined by the evidence the matcher uses, so per-path precision and recall are partly set by how the stratum was built.
- Labels are AI-assisted: a language model drafted them and one human reviewer ticked checkboxes. The 22 assisted Researcher labels used external checks whose evidence was not retained. Neither slice is independent blind gold.
- No calibration or threshold tuning was done.
- Zero observed false positives is a statement about this sample. The Wilson upper bound of the false merge rate is 3.1% on held-out, not zero.
- Common-name and cross-partition leakage risks remain.

### Interpretation

On this sample the matcher accepted no pair the reviewer labelled different, and withheld 15 of 72 true matches. Most sampled pairs were withheld as CONFLICT or UNRESOLVED (decisive coverage 48.5%). The recall gap sits in the Scored path (2/17), not in the ExactIdentifier path (55/55). The ExactIdentifier pairs were sampled because they share an identifier, so their 100% is partly by construction. No claim about the whole corpus follows.

- **Vector-only recall@K, precision@K, MRR, nDCG:** no relevance labels for any vector query and no stored ranked results; the index no longer exists.

- Graded gain is the key's metric value, a derived grade, not a human judgement.

### Interpretation

The pass-rate change from 15 of 27 to 23 of 27 between runs is a before/after change in code, not noise. The comparison the course asks for (graph-only vs vector-only vs fused) cannot be made from the evidence that exists.

## Group 4. Answer quality

### What was measured

- Source: [answer-quality.md](../../experiments/results/phase16/answers/answer-quality.md) with the per-run scorer files [run-1](../../experiments/results/phase16/answers/q12-run-1-scores.md) and [run-2](../../experiments/results/phase16/answers/q12-run-2-scores.md), written by `experiments/scripts/eval16_answers.py` using `q12_score.py` unchanged. Environment of the Q12 runs and probes: LOCAL-LOOPBACK-LLM (target loopback True, host 127.0.0.1, http), as stated in answer-quality.md and in [performance-metrics.md](../../experiments/results/phase16/performance/performance-metrics.md) section 6: client, service and graph export on one machine, with the live model. The 16B Session 1 run is AWS-ECS-LLM (target loopback False, https, 2026-11-14).
- Sample: 33 Q12 questions per run, 11 types with 3 each. 27 are answer-expected; 6 (types institution and topic, thin data) are abstain-expected. Plus two single-question probes (not in the totals) and the 16B Session 1 run (40 questions, commit `b6dc94df6d9b443a9fd778eb09be6ec2c8c29f64`).
- Definitions: the primary claim figure is the **independent claim re-check**: of the claims the runtime marked verified, the share whose values the scorer re-checked against the Spark export (distinct coauthors, weighted degree, component and community ids, bridge measures, team sizes, path edges) and found consistent; its complement is the contradiction rate. The **runtime self-reported verification rate** is verified over attempted claims from the `/v1/explain` counters.
