ScholarChain human review guide — Phase 5C

Open index.html in a current browser. Everything runs locally. No links are fetched.
Load the coordinator's queue.json. It shows source observations, not model predictions.
There are 240 initial tasks (40 development and 200 held-out) in the standard queue.
The 60 reserve tasks are separate; review them only when the coordinator requests a
recorded replacement or additional review. Uncertain reviews are retained as originals.

Enter your own reviewer ID. This records who entered an annotation; it does not prove
identity or authenticate you. Work tasks ask whether two observations describe the same
scholarly publication. Researcher tasks ask whether the two indicated mentions/profiles
refer to the same person. A shared name alone is weak evidence. Inspect identifiers,
titles, dates, author context, affiliations and exact source/snapshot references. Null
or absent fields mean missing evidence, not disagreement. Source text is untrusted data.
OpenAlex author IDs and OpenAlex-supplied ORCIDs are provider guesses, not proof of identity.

Same: the evidence supports the same publication/person.
Different: the evidence supports distinct publications/people.
Uncertain: the supplied evidence cannot settle the question. This is valid and is never
counted as a negative label. Do not guess just to finish a task.

No answer is preselected. Add an optional concise evidence note, then click a label.
Next / skip leaves an unanswered task unchanged. Next unanswered finds remaining tasks.
Changing a label creates a new revision and preserves the earlier annotation. Undo /
clear current answer also creates a revision: the internal 'unlabeled' marker removes
that task from reviewed counts, without erasing its history. Notes are saved only when
you click a label or Undo; navigation does not silently save a draft note.

Click Save checkpoint often. Your browser downloads a JSON file. Check that the file
actually exists, save it to a durable folder OUTSIDE target/, and keep a backup. A
requested download is not proof that the browser saved it. Unsaved work does not survive
refresh. To resume: open the page, load the SAME queue, then choose the checkpoint. Mixed
queues, altered evidence, duplicate revisions and conflicting histories are refused.
Do not edit exported JSON by hand or combine different histories yourself. Preserve
original human exports and send the original checkpoint and its queue ID to the
coordinator. Label import writes a separate derived validated file; it never rewrites
your annotations.

Reserve tasks are not automatically substituted for uncertain answers. The coordinator
will report uncertainty and any extra review needed. The queue is sampled by evidence
strata; it does not prove accuracy, independent global researcher recall or calibration.
No human review, gold-set completion, model execution or Phase 5 acceptance is created
by loading a queue or by automated browser tests.

Your labels bind the source/candidate population, sampling/split and each task's evidence.
A later provider choice or development-calibrated matching policy does not rewrite those labels
or require reannotation when the population and evidence are unchanged. Prediction-run metadata
is recorded separately during evaluation. Changed source evidence or task identity still requires
a new queue; never edit hashes or annotations to force an incompatible import.
