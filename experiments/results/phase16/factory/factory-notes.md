## Rules

- Verdicts: the upper-case tokens APPROVE, REQUEST-CHANGES, CHANGES-REQUIRED (so 'APPROVED_PLAN_SHA256' and lower-case 'approve' do not match); a token preceded within 40 characters by 'after reviewer' is a consent condition, not a verdict, and is skipped. Several verdicts on one line are each counted (r1, r2, r3).
- Human decisions: lines containing HUMAN DECISION, USER (verbatim), HUMAN (chat), HUMAN:, USER: or 'Human decision'.
- Guard blocks: the same pattern as the committed-record parser.

## Limitations

- The notes are a coordinator's running log, not an event log: a verdict, block or intervention that was not noted is not counted.
- The log is append-only and git-ignored: only its first 199 lines are read (its state when Phase 16 started; the sha256 is over those lines), later notes are not part of this evaluation; the output cannot be regenerated from a fresh clone.
